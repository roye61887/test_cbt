About TIT

TECHNO INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (TIT) is a premier educational and skill development institution founded with a clear commitment to delivering quality education, modern computer literacy, and transparent online assessment systems for students across the country and around the globe.

We believe that true education goes beyond textbooks. Alongside rigorous academic preparation and Computer-Based Testing (CBT) readiness for school and competitive students, TIT actively champions environmental sustainability through our Plastic Waste Management & Recycling project and promotes mental and physical harmony through structured Yoga training programs.

Our Mission

  • To provide affordable, high-quality education and modern digital examination exposure to every student regardless of geographical barriers.
  • To empower youth with practical technology skills, analytical confidence, and ethical values.
  • To contribute actively toward a cleaner environment through plastic recycling awareness and healthier lifestyles via regular yoga practice.

Our Vision

  • To emerge as a globally trusted center of excellence in digital education, online assessment, and holistic student development.
  • To bridge the rural-urban digital divide by bringing standardized CBT platforms directly to every learner's fingertips.
  • To inspire a generation of healthy, environmentally conscious, and future-ready achievers living up to our motto: "YOUR SUCCESS OUR GOAL".

  1. This Computer-Based Test (CBT) comprises 50 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) carrying 1 mark each (Total: 50 Marks).
